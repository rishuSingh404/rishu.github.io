I am a third-year undergraduate at IIT Patna working at the **AI-NLP-ML Research Lab** under Prof. Sriparna Saha and the **Video Surveillance and Biomedical Signal Processing Lab** under Prof. Maheshkumar H. Kolekar.

My research focuses on **mechanistic interpretability**, **multimodal reasoning**, and **AI safety** — understanding how vision-language models internally represent and route information, and building systems that are transparent and steerable.
